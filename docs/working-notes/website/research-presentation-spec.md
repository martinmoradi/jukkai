recommend what Jukkai's website should communicate, and show Crystelle how her
contributions will supply that content. She knows the practice but has little

lessons and concrete examples. The central connection is:

**Business question → evidence and reference examples → interpretation → proposed
content for Jukkai → existing material and remaining inputs.**

edition, particularly interior-architecture content and the eventual Studio
Terrasson transition. Architecture remains commercially central. Galerie already
has substantial magazine-edition work to build on and needs less preparation with
Crystelle. These materials explain and inform the next website work; they do not

## 5. Presentation and notes

Build an argument, not a run-by-run research recital. Establish the purpose and
breadth briefly, work through the consequential business questions with evidence
and references, then connect the lessons to proposed content and the dependency map.
Architecture receives most depth; Galerie receives a shorter, substantive treatment.

For each major topic, Martin should be able to explain:

1. What question was investigated and why it matters.
2. What was observed, with one or more revealing examples.
3. What that suggests, including an explicit recommendation for Jukkai.
4. What is already known or available.
5. What exact business choice, story, document or asset remains necessary.

This is a reasoning structure, not a compulsory five-box slide template. Let
worked examples span several slides where useful. One project may answer several
questions; some explanations come from mission documents or operational facts.

Speaker notes must be populated for substantive slides and aligned with the final
slide order. Provide the takeaway, natural talking points, explanations of figures
and reference annotations, relevant limitations, transitions and optional deeper
detail. Help Martin handle likely questions. Keep the primary spoken path around
40 minutes while making deeper discussion possible. Notes should support speech,
not merely repeat visible text or become an obligatory word-for-word script.

End with the method and content dependencies described below. The opening
presentation can lead to focused work over several calls; do not force all
interviews into the initial session.

## 6. Provisional sitemap and module dependencies

The closing must make this relationship concrete:

**Work to complete → usable content → proposed website destinations.**

destination map. A useful starting hypothesis is homepage, Architecture, Galerie,
Contact and selected project stories. Conseil/professional content may be sections
or distinct destinations. Preserve that uncertainty; do not freeze slugs, navigation
or one page per sector/keyword. Show what the destinations must communicate and
how content connects, rather than presenting URL nesting alone.

Modules are bounded pieces of work with a recognizable output, not broad themes
or mandatory interview chapters. Present the prepared organization as work Martin
owns and will adapt. Crystelle supplies professional substance, business decisions
and material within it; she is not being asked to design or approve the module system.

Prepare the initial mapping from the existing sources. For each module record its
purpose/output, known inputs, specific gaps, useful activity/contributor, and the
content/destinations it supplies. Activities may include interviews, reflection,
files/documents, factual confirmation or Martin's own editorial/technical work.
Do not re-interview Crystelle for facts already sufficiently documented.

Represent many-to-many relationships: one module can feed several destinations;
one destination can depend on several modules. A missing input affects its dependent
content, not every part of the website. Distinguish available information from
remaining work. A documented unresolved decision can be a useful interview outcome.

Use a legible overview and one expanded example in the main deck; retain fuller
mapping in its appendix. An illustrative example is clarifying mission boundaries,
documenting a professional project's users/constraints/choices, and gathering its
photos/plans, which together feed professional explanations and a project story.
The agent must populate the delivered map with actual starting material and gaps.

Account for the content and readiness needs through the eventual full-site launch:
identity/people; desired residential/professional work; project stories and assets;
missions/responsibilities; works and fees; trust/proof; first contact and reach;
Galerie selection/buying/visits; current administrative/publication information;
old content/identity continuity; and Martin-owned website verification/publication.
Keep technical coverage compact and show business inputs only where they matter.
This is coverage and dependency information, not a calendar, development backlog,
timed roadmap or commitment to finish every enrichment before launch.

The report may discuss sitemap/content implications supported by research. It must
not contain this module mapping or the collaboration method.

precise local source identifiers in the production handoff. Never expose credentials
or private raw exports through public sharing to make citations work.
