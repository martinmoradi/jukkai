## Maintaining the working set

Keep unique business context and evidence in the document that captures it, with
links from the plan and copy. Do not paste the entire research synthesis into a
page brief, or repeat navigation decisions in every draft. If two documents cease
to have distinct jobs, consolidate their unique material before archiving the older one.

The direct September 10 account of budget concerns is recorded in the Architecture
proof brief; it must not disappear when shortening proposals. It remains distinct
from agent-authored explanations, invented examples and approved public claims.

When content is accepted, record the decision in its existing ticket and update
the owning canonical document or implementation handoff as appropriate. Link the
accepted destination from the draft, then archive superseded proposals. Preserve
alternatives still under discussion rather than declaring them rejected by relocation.
