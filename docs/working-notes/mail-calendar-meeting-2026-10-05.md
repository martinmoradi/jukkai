# Mail and calendar transition — 5 October 2026

Internal meeting notes with Crystelle, Laura and Martin. Records the current
setup and what matters for the transition; not an approved technical plan.
Revisit when preparing the proposed setup, after checking the actual accounts.

## Direction

- Move from Studio Terrasson to Jukkai addresses while preserving correspondence
  and continuity. The current organization broadly works; this is an opportunity
  to improve it, not rebuild everything.
- Keep one individual mailbox each and easy access to selected shared incoming mail.
- Aim for two paid users in the existing Workspace organization. Avoid a third
  permanent license just for generic addresses or occasional interns.
- Both are open to trying Gmail instead of Thunderbird.
- Martin will handle setup, migration, on-site onboarding and training.

## Individual address names — open

  `c.terrasson@jukkai.fr` instead of `ct@jukkai.fr`.
- Crystelle and Laura are open to this, following his suggestion. They have not
  experienced problems with their initials-based addresses; this is not a need
  they raised themselves. No naming format agreed yet.
- Agent suggestion for discussion: `crystelle@jukkai.fr` and `laura@jukkai.fr`
  suit a small, personal practice and are easy to say. Initial + surname is a
  more formal alternative. Preserve existing addresses as receiving aliases
  where applicable if the primary addresses change.

## Calendar and contacts — Karlia

- Used for calendar and contacts, not much else as a CRM.
- Source of truth for client contact details: name, address, email, phone and
  sometimes acquisition source, such as a referral network.
- Appointments link to clients, providing a history Laura occasionally consults
  and values.
- Shared calendar view with two profiles that can be shown or hidden.
- Crystelle has full control. Laura can create appointments for Crystelle but
  cannot subsequently edit/delete them. These defaults work fine; reproducing
  the exact permissions is not a stated requirement.
- Calendly bookings appear automatically in Karlia. Karlia appointments also
  block Calendly availability.
- Categories can be applied after a Calendly booking or to manually created
  appointments: phone calls, chantier, lunch, teaching, suppliers, Jukkai events,
  office appointments, private/other, client visits, diagnostics with contractors,
  prospects, networking/trade fairs, video meetings and artists.
- Private appointments use the pink "other" category to indicate unavailability
  without details. This describes their usage, not a verified privacy setting.
