## What research should help us decide

Martin wants organised SEO research that makes the sitemap and copy easier to plan.
The magazine release is soon; the official place opening is October. We can understand
more of the eventual site than we publish initially.

Current questions, to sharpen with Martin before specifying fresh runs:

- What should the first homepage communicate about Jukkai, the established practice,
  the Galerie and the opening, using real available proof?
- Which audience/search needs deserve a distinct page, and which can be served well
  in the homepage initially? How do available content and editorial intent change that?
- Which architecture, project or Galerie pages are valuable next additions?
- What old-site queries, links and content need continuity through a later migration?
- How should the actual business move, settled name and October opening be represented
  through the website, existing Business Profile and external profiles?

The exact scope, fresh query set, output format and budget remain open. A release/later
sitemap and section briefs are an agent recommendation, not an accepted contract.
Do not silently shrink all SEO work to technical checks or remove Martin's broader
research interest; organise it so the first release can proceed.
