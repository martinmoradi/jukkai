# Jukkai planning and delivery method

Updated 2026-09-07 following Martin's delivery reset. Start with
[current delivery](current-delivery.md) for milestones and scope, and
[the foundation](../strategy/foundation.md) for settled business truth.
The July working prototype is archived as provenance; its exhaustive research gate
and fixed separation of planning from all building no longer govern delivery.

## Orient, then choose the next useful work

Martin should understand what a question controls before being asked to decide it.
Explain the important layers, dependencies, unknowns and evidence limits. Distinguish
observations, interpretations, hypotheses, recommendations, working preferences and
settled decisions. Hedged assent permits exploration, not silent formalisation.

The site model may look beyond the next release. The next release only needs enough
clarity to publish its selected content well. Scope research around questions that
can change the work; name the output, evidence source, stopping point and any paid
budget before a run. Do not repeat broad setup/audits when usable evidence exists.

A practical loop is:

1. Identify the visitor/business job and current delivery constraint.
2. Read settled facts and available content; identify the decision still open.
3. Gather only the evidence that can materially help that decision.
4. Compare concrete options with Martin, using rough copy or a prototype when useful.
5. Record the decision in its ticket, then define the corresponding implementation
   handoff or update the appropriate canonical document.
6. Build and verify the approved slice; publish through the normal release process.
7. Revisit later pages and experiments as new content or evidence arrives.

These are reasoning steps, not mandatory separate documents or sequential phases
for the entire website. Independent technical preparation or visual exploration can
proceed with explicit assumptions while unrelated research continues. Never publish
an unapproved business promise or treat a prototype as accepted design.

## Evidence and authority

Martin owns website, marketing and design decisions, using his knowledge of Crystelle
and Jukkai. Consult Crystelle for a new or uncertain business commitment Martin cannot
confidently represent. Prepare recommendations and alternatives, rather than handing
her raw data or making all content depend on a comprehensive questionnaire.

Search results and competitors inform opportunities and constraints; they do not
choose pages, offers or creative trade-offs. Vendor estimates and audit scores stay
labelled. Historical measurements retain date, population and coverage limits.
External providers own their procedures: Google's profile eligibility is distinct
from Martin's settled brand choice. Check those procedures before acting; do not
turn an unverified provider outcome into a canonical guarantee.

## Enough clarity for a selected page

Before its implementation handoff, make the page's purpose, audience, truthful
message, available proof/assets and useful action understandable. Identify important
discovery, accessibility, metadata, responsive and production requirements. Record
what is open and where design has freedom. A short section brief can hold this;
there is no compulsory nine-column page table or keyword-to-page formula.

A missing input blocks the content that depends on it, not every other page.
Additional pages need ready content and review capacity as well as cheap code.

## Artifact ownership

- **Current delivery:** active scope, milestones and standing delivery boundaries.
- **Foundation and its derived adapter:** business truth, brand and claims.
- **Wayfinder map:** destination and links to decisions, open child issues and fog.
  Follow [tracker operations](../agents/issue-tracker.md). Do not duplicate ticket
  answers into another evolving roadmap.
- **Inquiry/evidence:** useful questions and source limitations; not canonical
  strategy or an exhaustive prerequisite to building.
- **SEO log:** concise current synthesis and a run index. Detailed reviewed
  observations and corrections live in linked run records, not repeated in every
  brief. The capability map separately owns command routing.
- **Implementation issues:** approved behavior, dependencies and verification.
- **Working notes:** provisional session captures, clearly labelled as such.
  The [website working set](../working-notes/website/README.md) routes visitor
  explanations, page alternatives, plans and draft copy by their distinct purpose.

Keep this system small. Update the relevant document when a decision crystallises;
create another artifact only for a real coordination need. Do not reopen settled
choices because an older issue, tool recommendation or agent template says otherwise.
Follow the [documentation map](../README.md#keep-docs-fresh) when updating authority,
retiring a draft or moving files. Organization and review labels do not approve a
business promise, page model or design.
