## Goal and segment

Make the Galerie visitable and desirable: what you will find when you push the door,
how buying works, and when you can come. This is the site's first secondary job
([foundation §3](../strategy/foundation.md#3-website-jobs)). It serves art visitors,
not an architecture segment. For architecture clients it is a halo, never a
cross-sell ([§7](../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture)).

**Current state (Martin, 2026-10-08):** open, but still settling in. The artworks
have arrived and been installed, Crystelle is starting to work on Saturdays, and it
should be in full swing within a few weeks. Hours are not confirmed yet.

## Visitor question

« Qu'est-ce que je vais découvrir, est-ce que je peux l'acheter, et quand puis-je venir ? »

## What it must prove

- Real works, and the reasons Crystelle chose them.
- A welcoming, lively place, not a hushed gallery.
- Buying is simple: how to enquire, buy and take a work home.
- Practical visit information that is true today.

## Constraints

- [Foundation §9](../strategy/foundation.md#9-the-galerie-promise-pre-opening) still
  applies until #150 revisits it for an open Galerie: no art prices, no event
  promises, no artist names without consent.
- Never: a catalogue, ecommerce or investment claims
  ([§9](../strategy/foundation.md#9-the-galerie-promise-pre-opening), [§10](../strategy/foundation.md#10-proof-bank)).
- `Galerie` is the category; avoid boutique and catalogue codes
  ([§11](../strategy/foundation.md#11-voice-and-public-french)). The thesis line is
  Crystelle's to validate.
- One quiet « vous êtes artiste ? » route, with no dedicated page.
