# Contact

**Status:** live. **URL:** `/contact/`. **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-13.

## Goal and segment

Turn intent into a conversation, for both activities, without blurring them: an
architecture enquiry is not a Galerie visit or an artwork question. Every segment
ends here; a contact is the site's conversion
([foundation §3](../strategy/foundation.md#3-website-jobs)).

**Re-mapped (Martin, 2026-10-12/13):** Galerie visit information lives on the
[Galerie page](galerie.md); this page is the contact path for projects. Its main call
to action books a first call through the existing Calendly, with a form for those who
prefer one. The phone stays reachable but less prominent; an office number with
hours comes later. Crystelle's yes is pending in
[Questions for Crystelle](https://github.com/martinmoradi/jukkai/issues/165).

## Constraints

- Never hide the phone or force a channel for cleaner analytics
  ([§3](../strategy/foundation.md#3-website-jobs), [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-cookieless-first-party-analytics.md)).
- The free first meeting comes after Crystelle has qualified the lead: no instant
  booking promise ([§7](../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture)). A booked
  call is that qualification step, so its copy never promises the meeting.
- Rennes is the commercial claim and Châteaugiron the physical anchor
  ([§8](../strategy/foundation.md#8-seo-posture-and-ia-confidence-gate)).
- The printed `/c/crystelle` pointer stays locked
  ([contact-card guide](../operations/crystelle-contact-card.md)).
