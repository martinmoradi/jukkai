# Contact

**Status:** live. **URL:** `/contact/`. **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08.

## Goal and segment

Turn intent into a conversation, for both activities, without blurring them: an
architecture enquiry is not a Galerie visit or an artwork question. Every segment
ends here; a contact is the site's conversion
([foundation §3](../strategy/foundation.md#3-website-jobs)).

## Visitor question

« Comment vous joindre, que se passe-t-il ensuite, et puis-je passer ? »

## What it must prove

- Current, verified channels and address, with the phone easy to reach.
- What happens after a first call for an architecture project.
- Separate, truthful Galerie visit information.
