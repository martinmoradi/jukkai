## Goal and segment

Show that Crystelle's eye appeals and that her work answers situations like the
visitor's. Jukkai shows its curated past projects itself. Ambitious projects
attract the ceiling segment; a deliberate share of residential work keeps
mid-budget missions welcome. Every project links on to the interior-design page
and to contact.

## Two formats, one URL (Martin, 2026-10-08)

1. **Réalisation, now:** verified facts, strong images, a short text and links.
   It is honest about what we know and indexable through its descriptive title.
2. **Récit, later:** a session with Crystelle grows the same page into a case
   study answering one visitor question. [Le Capri](le-capri.md) is the first.

## Visitor question

« Son travail me plaît-il, et a-t-elle déjà résolu une situation comme la mienne ? »

## Rules (Martin, 2026-10-08)

- **Titles:** the H1 says what the project is. A creative name survives only as an
  eyebrow, and only when it names something real (a period, an object, a design
  move). Titles never identify a private client: the old names existed for that.
- **Images by role,** not projects by tier: _hero_ (clean, at least 2400 px),
  _gallery_ (at least 1300 px, straight, uncluttered), _detail_ and _astuce_ (small,
  any sharp image of a strong design). Revisit as masters turn up.
- **Masters first (Martin, 2026-10-11):** draft each réalisation from its project's
  whole master batch ([project masters](../../../media/README.md#project-masters)),
  not by porting the old page. The old page's image choices, captions and texts are
  reference, not truth. Crystelle reviews the draft on a preview page.
- **Image rights (Martin, 2026-10-11):** Crystelle holds usage rights for every
  image in the project master batches. Credits still apply.
- **Watermarked old-site images** remain a fallback only for a project without
  masters.
- **Years** are always recorded, and shown only where they serve: recent work,
  repeat clients, any figure.
- **The index is curated** and leads with the best work; recency is one factor
  among others (Martin, 2026-10-11, revising « a recent project leading »). No
  particulier / professionnel split.
- **3D renders,** once Crystelle supplies them, may sit beside photos, labelled.
- Old captions are sources: their « astuces » become problem-led material, not text.
