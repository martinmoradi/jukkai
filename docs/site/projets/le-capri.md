## Goal and segment

The first project story. It sets the template the later stories reuse. It shows B2B
work Crystelle wants more of (ceiling segment): a restaurant that came back to her
about ten years after the earlier _Waouh_ intervention. The new work leads; _Waouh_
becomes a short continuity chapter, not a second page
([foundation §10](../../strategy/foundation.md#10-proof-bank)).

## Questions it can answer

- **Visitor:** a restaurateur, most likely sent by a referral: « A-t-elle déjà conçu
  un restaurant comme le mien, de quoi s'est-elle chargée, et qu'est-ce que ça a changé ? »
- **Search:** restaurant design in and around Rennes (architecte d'intérieur restaurant,
  agencement, aménagement). Search volume is unmeasured, so the referred visitor
  comes first and search is a bonus.

## What it must prove

- Repeat trust: the same client returning a decade later.
- Restaurant constraints understood: the activity, staff, guests and atmosphere.
- Crystelle's responsibility and the result.
