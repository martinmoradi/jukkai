## Goal and segment

The commercial centre ([foundation §5](../../strategy/foundation.md#5-center-of-gravity-the-two-layers-and-the-open-ia-seam)).
It answers whether Crystelle is the right interior architect for a project and how
working together goes. It is also the main destination for local service searches
(Rennes is the claim, Châteaugiron the anchor, [§8](../../strategy/foundation.md#8-seo-posture-and-ia-confidence-gate)).
All three segments read it, each for a different job: the ceiling is reassured by
responsibility proof and projects, mid-budget missions meet expectations before
the first meeting, and advice comes after the proof.
