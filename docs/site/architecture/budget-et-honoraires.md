## Goal and segment

Set expectations before the first meeting, mainly for mid-budget full missions:
the same envelope has to cover necessary underlying work, visible transformation
and the mission's fees. Because fees follow the works while effort follows
complexity, a clear explanation filters and prepares enquiries rather than
discouraging them.

## Visitor question

« Comment mon budget, les travaux et vos honoraires s'articulent-ils ? »

## What it must prove

- **The fee basis (settled):** studies and maîtrise d'œuvre cost 12–16 % of the
  estimated works budget, HT ([§7](../../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture)).
- **The expectation gap:** electricity, plumbing or insulation can take a large share
  before any visible change. Budget is the main concern in recent enquiries
  (Martin, [architecture brief](../../working-notes/website/architecture-content-proof-brief.md#what-the-research-adds)).
- **One real story** where the fundamentals reshaped the envelope, and the choice the
  mission helped make.
- **What happens after a first call.**

## Constraints

- The old « avant-projet à partir de 700 € HT » is never published.
- No standard allocation, savings promise or fee that "pays for itself".
- Historical euros are not current prices; recent figures need a date and scope and
  Crystelle's agreement ([§7](../../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture)).
- No simulator or cost guide in this map (out of scope).
