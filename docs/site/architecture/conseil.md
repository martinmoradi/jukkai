# Architecture — Conseil / mission déco

**Status:** waiting on [#145](https://github.com/martinmoradi/jukkai/issues/145).
**URL:** a section of [Architecture](index.md); a page of its own only if it earns one.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08.

## Goal and segment

Promote the advice and sourcing offer as a named, bounded mission, with
the best fee-to-effort ratio of the three segments. It sits **after**
the responsibility proof and is never the front door. It reads as an architecte
d'intérieur's expertise (her eye, her selection, her sourcing), framed as
« une mission déco avec l'exigence d'une architecte d'intérieur »
([§7](../../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture), approved by Crystelle in July).

## Visitor question

« J'ai besoin d'un regard et de choix précis, pas d'une rénovation complète :
pouvez-vous m'aider, avec quoi je repars, et combien ça coûte ? »

## What it must prove

- The offer exists, with a name.
- Concrete deliverables and clear boundaries.
- A price floor.
- An example of what the eye and the sourcing changed.

## Constraints

- The identity never changes: she is always an architecte d'intérieur; only the mission
  scope does ([§7, the décoratrice tension](../../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture)).
- Name, inclusions and fees need Crystelle's operational validation before copy.
- No named art-placement service ([§7](../../strategy/foundation.md#7-offer-architecture)).
