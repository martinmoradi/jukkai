# Home

**Status:** live (magazine edition); target story **untracked**. **URL:** `/`.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-08.

## Goal and segment

Tell the Jukkai story: how an interior architect's practice became a place that also
holds a Galerie, and what Jukkai does. Architecture stays the commercial centre inside
that story ([foundation §5](../strategy/foundation.md#5-center-of-gravity-the-two-layers-and-the-open-ia-seam)),
then each visitor is routed to the page that answers their question. All segments
pass through here; it is mainly the referral visitor who heard a name and searched for it.

**Tentative (Martin, 2026-10-08):** storytelling and a beautiful presentation, not a
catalogue of what Jukkai does. The hard part is presenting two activities neatly.
Working shape: a hero, then what Jukkai is, then an interior-design section and a
Galerie section, each with its call to action, then a sample of existing Google
review excerpts. The reviews are for trust, not search: Google shows no review
stars for a business's own reviews on its own site.

## Visitor question

« Qu'est-ce que Jukkai, est-ce bien la Crystelle dont on m'a parlé, et où trouver
ce qui me concerne ? »

## What it must prove

- **Continuity:** Studio Terrasson continues as Jukkai. It is not a closed practice
  and not a career change.
- **The practice is the core:** interior architecture is current, substantial and
  responsible, not an aside next to the art.
- **The Galerie is real and open,** and belongs to the same eye (vision only).
- **There is a clear way forward** to architecture, the Galerie or contact.
