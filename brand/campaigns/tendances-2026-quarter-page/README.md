## From the media kit

Source: [`tendances-media-kit-2024.pdf`](tendances-media-kit-2024.pdf), the
publisher's "Contraintes techniques — formats 2024" sheet (InDesign export dated
2023-11-28). It is the 2024 edition; whether it still applies in 2026 is unconfirmed.

- **1/4 page: 88 × 126 mm, no bleed.** Same height as the October half page
  (180 × 126), less than half its width.
- **Reserved for « shopping » themes**, published in the November and December
  issues (end-of-year holidays), May (Mother's Day) or summer (summer shopping).
- **Files:** JPG at 300 dpi or high-definition PDF, CMYK. Accepted: JPG, TIFF, PDF,
  AI, PSD, InDesign; no Word, PowerPoint or Publisher.
- Other formats, for comparison: double page 420 × 297 and full page 210 × 297 (both
  +5 mm bleed, 5 mm safety margin), half page 180 × 126 without bleed. The
  « confidentiel / tiers de page » insertion follows a separate publisher charter.

## Open questions for the grilling session

- Which issue, and what is the material deadline?
- What does Jukkai offer that fits a « shopping » page: artworks as gifts, a
  gift card, an artist, an event? Is that a business claim the
  [foundation](../../../docs/strategy/foundation.md) already supports?
- Does Tendances lay the quarter pages out together on a themed shopping page,
  and does that change how the ad must read next to its neighbours?
- Continue the October system (photo-led, Voyage headline, navy fade) or depart
  from it? At 88 mm, the October headline size and 6 mm insets will not fit as-is.
- Which photograph, whose voice in the copy, and what call to action?
- Who approves: Crystelle, Martin, or both?
